# Grader: no cooking over a Senzing that will not stand up

SKILL.md is explicit: when Senzing can't deploy, hand off to `install` — "don't cook over a
Senzing that won't stand up." A run that starts mapping the ingredients anyway (papering over the
missing SDK) fails this grader — it is the obligation's own negative form, so it fails on the
one behavior it exists to forbid.
